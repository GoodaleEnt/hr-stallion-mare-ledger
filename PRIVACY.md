# Privacy Policy — HR Stallion & Mare Ledger

_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

HR Stallion & Mare Ledger is a browser extension that records your own Horse Reality game data in a private ledger on your computer.

## What it stores

- Stallion, mare and breeding records, stud fees (in-game currency), foal results and horse details (names, breed, genetics, pedigree) taken from horsereality.com pages you view.
- Horse portraits from Horse Reality, saved as images.
- Your own notes and tags (status, role, project, purchase prices, scores) and the Horse Reality username you enter in Settings.

All of this is kept in your browser's local extension storage. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, and the extension has no account, analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What it connects to

- **horsereality.com** — it reads the pages you open and asks Horse Reality's own API for details of the horse you are viewing, using your existing Horse Reality login in your browser.
- **Google Fonts** — the dashboard page loads its fonts from Google, which means Google receives your IP address with that request, as with any web font.

## Sharing and sale

Your data is never sold, shared or used for anything other than showing you your ledger.

## Your control

Use **Export Backup** in the dashboard to save your data as a file, or remove the extension (or clear its data) to delete everything it has stored.
